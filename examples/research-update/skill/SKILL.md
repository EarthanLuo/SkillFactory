# Research Update

## Use when

Preparing a routine research discussion, progress update, anomaly review, or decision request.

## Do not use as-is when

The meeting is a formal complete review with explicit reporting requirements. In that case, satisfy those requirements first, then use this Skill to highlight decisions and anomalies.

## Inputs

- meeting purpose;
- audience familiarity;
- current status and most important anomaly or uncertainty;
- current interpretation;
- proposed next step or decision needed.

## Method

1. Choose context by audience: for a familiar listener, use at most one or two sentences; for an unfamiliar listener, provide only the minimum needed to interpret the current issue.
2. State the highest-value anomaly or uncertainty before listing routine activity.
3. Give the current judgment or interpretation, separating observation from inference.
4. State the next experiment, decision, or question.
5. Add routine progress only when it changes the decision or prevents a misleading impression.

## Output shape

Use:

`minimal context → anomaly/uncertainty → current judgment → next step or decision`

Keep the update concrete. If there is no meaningful anomaly, say what decision the status is intended to support instead of inventing one.
