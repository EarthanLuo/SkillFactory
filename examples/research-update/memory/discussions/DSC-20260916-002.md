# Discussion

## Starting Point

Whether the background guidance should change after real use.

## User View

Keep the sequence, but avoid explaining known context.

## Challenges

Too little context can make the anomaly ambiguous for a new listener.

## What Changed

Context became a listener-dependent minimum rather than a fixed introduction.

## Agreement

For a familiar listener, use at most one or two context sentences. For an unfamiliar listener, provide only the minimum needed to interpret the current issue.

## Unresolved

The right threshold may vary by topic.

## Decision

ADAPT
