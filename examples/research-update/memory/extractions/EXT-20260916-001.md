# Extraction

## Problem

A chronological update can hide the issue that most needs research judgment.

## Approach

Lead with the anomaly, explain the current interpretation, then ask for or propose the next decision.

## Reasoning

Unexpected results often carry more decision value than a list of completed tasks.

## Conditions

The listener has enough project context, and the meeting is primarily for diagnosis or decision-making rather than a formal complete review.

## Hidden Assumptions

The anomaly is real and selected well; the listener can understand it with little setup; routine progress is less important than the decision.

## Transferable Experience

For decision-oriented research conversations, prioritize the highest-value uncertainty over chronology.

## Uncertainty

The rule may be wrong for onboarding, formal evaluations, or listeners unfamiliar with the project.

## Possible Skill

An advisor-update Skill that selects context and ordering by audience and meeting purpose.
